\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The main question asked to what extent a bounded Docker NDT can enforce and measure controlled conditions while reusing one validated pipeline across topology and guarded automation extensions. The answer is conditional but positive within the tested configurations. For RQ1, delay and loss had significant overall associations with measured RTT and loss, while bandwidth measurements showed that configured rates are reference targets rather than end-to-end values. For RQ2, the pipeline extended through two routers to a 50-node/88-link Germany50 instantiation; the 4,224-entry plan was dry-run validated, and real traffic covered three representative, confounded path cases. For RQ3, guarded Qwen-compatible generation and 80 measured control rows reused the pipeline; official OpenAI returned HTTP 429, and Q-learning showed no path switch across its 20 single-seed rows. Those Q-learning rows span a migration and agent reinitialization rather than one uninterrupted learning trajectory.

The contribution is a reproducibility-oriented experimental integration, not a validated replica of a physical network. Shared-host effects, small cohorts, representative Germany50 coverage, provider availability, historical Dashboard evidence gaps, and one learner formulation bound the conclusion. Traceability remains a methodology and audit property, not an independently quantified success claim. Future work should test independent hosts and broader traffic coverage while preserving the sealed evidence.
